# salon-beauty — design reference notes

15 kept out of 23 candidates captured (hair salon, nail salon, spa, barbershop all represented).
Strongest-hit-rate category so far — beauty roundup sites skew genuinely custom-built far more
than home-services roundups do.

- **andy-lecompte-salon-full.png** — Best in the folder. Black/gold/cream palette, diagonal
  photo-vs-text section splits (not a plain stacked grid), press-logo row (Vogue, Elle, Allure),
  a lifestyle "Our Promise" shot of coffee + donuts instead of another salon photo, black-and-white
  celebrity-stylist portrait. This is what "one deliberate brand carried through every section"
  looks like at its best.
- **ama-the-salon-full.png** — Editorial art-gallery salon interior (real photo, vintage
  framed art on the walls), bold alternating color-block sections (terracotta/green/cream), real
  black-and-white portrait photography. Full of specific personality — nothing generic-template
  about it.
- **jocelyn-petroni-full.png** — Spa/skincare. Full-bleed cinematic water/swim photography,
  minimal serif type, near-zero UI chrome. Proof that restraint + one strong photography concept
  can carry an entire premium page with almost no "sections" at all.
- **fabio-doti-salon-full.png** — Financial District NYC hair salon. Black-and-white editorial
  hero, bold serif headline type, a 4-tile glamour-photography grid (Womens/Mens/Color/Treatments)
  used as the services nav instead of icon cards, real luxury-brand logo row (Oribe, Kérastase).
- **the-cosmo-studio-full.png** — Full-bleed real interior photo of the actual nail studio
  (not stock), dense authentic photo grid of real nail art + real product photography (hand
  sanitizer). Best reference in the folder for "photo density = trust."
- **butterfly-studio-salon-full.png** — Real salon interior photography, orange butterfly
  logo mark, warm gold hand-drawn brush-stroke label under "Team" — small hand-made touches like
  that brush stroke are what separate this from a stock template.
- **hiroshi-beverly-hills-full.png** — Japanese-inspired hero collage + a genuinely unusual
  stylist-card system: every stylist gets a nickname/label ("The King of Precision Cuts," "The
  Modern Artisan") illustrated like a trading card. Strong example of personality-driven,
  hand-built information design applied to a "meet the team" section, which is usually the most
  generic part of any service site.
- **grand-noir-salon-full.png** — Dark teal/mint minimal palette, elegant serif logo, real
  testimonial photos in speech-bubble callouts, "text us" CTA instead of just phone/book —
  distinct, cohesive color system on a fairly simple layout.
- **artiva-nails-full.png** — Real salon photography throughout, warm rose/burgundy palette,
  "Voted Best Nail Salon" award badge used as a real trust signal (not a stock badge row), full
  testimonial block styled as a pull-quote rather than a card.
- **jinsoon-full.png** — DTC nail-polish product brand, not a salon, but the editorial
  product photography (macro shots, styled flat-lays), press-logo row (Vogue, Allure, Forbes),
  and clean grid are the right register for a premium beauty aesthetic.
- **miniluxe-full.png** — DTC nail-care brand. Real, diverse group team photo used for an
  "Empowerment is Beautiful" brand-story section — a good model for a team/culture section that
  isn't generic headshots-in-a-row.
- **paintbox-full.png** — Press-ons + nail-art studio. "As Seen In" logo wall (Vogue, Allure,
  Brides, Elle, Cosmopolitan, Glamour), a labelled award callout ("Best Press Ons by
  Cosmopolitan"), real studio interior photo with a spiral staircase — very premium NYC-studio
  feel.
- **butchers-eco-salon-full.png** — Warm terracotta/cream palette, diamond monogram logo,
  three real location photos with text overlays used as a "choose your location" nav instead of a
  plain list, real founder photo. Good multi-location layout reference.
- **fringe-hair-salon-full.png** — Mint/coral palette, honeycomb texture section for
  testimonials (a distinctive background treatment instead of a plain color block), real service
  photography grid.
- **cuts-and-bruises-barbershop-full.png** — Kept despite a broken "Our Work" gallery section
  (empty on capture) because the vintage-poster wordmark treatment and black/white/red barbershop
  identity is genuinely distinct hand-built branding — the only barbershop example sourced, worth
  a manual recapture later to confirm the gallery section actually renders.

## Rejected
Broken/incomplete captures were dropped outright regardless of visible quality (Salon SCK, Nail
Garden, Yello Salon, The Clip Joint, Toni&Guy all had large blank/unloaded sections). Drybar was
bot-blocked (Cloudflare challenge page only). IGK Hair captured but cut off after the hero/press
bar — likely lazy-load never triggered. The Roose was rejected on merit: a real site, but a bare
Vagaro-powered price list with zero photography, not a design reference.
