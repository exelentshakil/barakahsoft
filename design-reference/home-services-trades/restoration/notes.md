# restoration — design reference notes

15 kept out of 27 attempted candidates (1 candidate DNS-failed before it could even be judged:
austin-water-damage-restoration). Sourced via a mix of Google Maps-style "best water/fire
damage restoration" city searches and "best restoration company websites" agency/aggregator
roundups, spread across FL, CO, TX, CA, AZ, WI, KS/MO, AL/TN/KY/MS, WA, and NV/OH/CA (multi-
state). Sourcing was open to any real business worldwide with a genuine, premium, hand-built
site — quality and design discipline were the bar, not geography — though every kept site in
this trade happened to be US-based. This was the hardest of the three trades to source: a much
higher rate of Cloudflare/anti-bot walls, dead/parked domains, and broken lazy-load captures
than either homebuilders or remodelers, plus several strong-looking candidates turned out to be
big multi-state franchise rollups that read too generic/corporate for the "hand-built premium"
bar once inspected closely.

## Rejected but worth knowing why
This trade had by far the highest rate of **broken/blank lazy-load captures** of the three:
**vice-restoration**, **revival-restoration**, **biodynamic-restoration**,
**upper-manhattan-mold-damage**, and **ec-restoration-services** all had genuinely real,
well-branded content in the sections that did render (real crew photos, named reviews,
certification badges) but the full-page capture came back with large solid-color blank blocks
— sometimes more than half the page — consistent with scroll-triggered animations not firing
in time rather than an actual design flaw. Worth a re-capture with a longer settle time if this
trade is revisited; several of these looked like they'd have been strong keeps.
**united-water-restoration-memphis** (United Water Restoration Group) was polished and
real — a genuine video, real fleet photo, real stats — but reads as a large multi-state
franchise locator page (a full 50-state directory list dominates a big chunk of the page)
rather than a distinctive local business identity, so it was dropped for reading too
generic/corporate against the "hand-built premium" bar. **911-restoration-glendale** is a real,
established local franchise location but the layout leans on dense, dated SEO-style copy
blocks rather than a modern visual design system.

Five candidates were bot-blocked, dead, or otherwise inaccessible and not retried since the
trade already had a healthy count: **all-things-restored** and **complete-restoration-san-
antonio** (Cloudflare/bot-check interstitials), **blusky-restoration** (Cloudflare "performing
security verification"), **core-one-restoration** (domain expired, now a parked GoDaddy page),
and **pacific-pro-restoration** (Squarespace "Private Site" — access disabled by the owner).
**austin-water-damage-restoration** failed outright with a DNS resolution error before it could
even be screenshotted.
