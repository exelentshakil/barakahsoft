# plumbers — design reference notes

17 kept out of 42 candidates captured (1 pre-existing + 41 sourced via roundup articles +
agency portfolio mining). Real businesses only. Geography was intentionally not a filter here —
the brief is to source the best-designed real sites regardless of country, so this set spans NY,
NJ, GA, CA, TX, AK, OK, WA, MN, CT-adjacent New England, plus the UK, Canada, and Australia
where the design quality earned it.

- **quix-plumbing-service-full.png** — NYC (all five boroughs). Purple/orange, real branded van
  and technician photo, subscription membership tiers (Quix Care/Care+) used instead of discount
  coupons — a genuinely modern differentiator — real named reviews, borough-specific skyline
  icons. Near-benchmark polish, arguably the strongest single find in this set.
- **fortified-plumbing-full.png** — Lynden, Ontario, Canada. Elegant editorial bathroom/kitchen
  renovation photography, a real family story, restrained minimal typography. One of the most
  visually refined captures across all three trades in this pass — kept despite being outside the
  US on the strength of the design alone.
- **east-and-gray-full.png** — Harpenden, UK (since 1953). Real technician photo, a real 6-photo
  gallery of actual bathroom/boiler installations, real named reviews with photos, navy/white,
  clean and minimal. "3000+ Happy Customers" stat block feels earned given the real work shown.
- **park-lane-plumbing-full.png** — Melbourne, Australia. Short but tasteful — moody dark real
  bathroom/pressure-gauge/pipe photography, a blue-green flame-drop icon mark, a real client
  quote. Deliberate, minimal, no template tells.
- **half-moon-plumbing-full.png** — Tulsa, OK. Bold black background with hot-pink/lime-green
  accent — a genuinely distinctive departure from the usual blue/red home-services palette. Real
  gritty under-house jobsite photos (not staged), real crew photos, and a real local sponsorship
  ("Official Plumbing Partners of FC Tulsa").
- **joe-rushing-plumbing-full.png** — Lubbock, TX (South Plains, since 1948, four generations).
  Real vintage black-and-white founder photo alongside a real current-generation family photo,
  real technician-at-work photos, real 4.7-star/396+ review stat, service-area map with pins.
  Navy/gold, disciplined.
- **chambliss-plumbing-full.png** — San Antonio, TX (35+ years). Deep red/black/white, two
  distinct real crew group photos (spanning years), a real owner quote, real family-with-dog
  photo, a real job-photo grid (leak detection, drain cleaning, RO systems), manufacturer badges

## Rejected but worth knowing why
Cartoon mascots were the single most common "AI slop" tell in this trade: Mahi Plumbing's
cartoon fish ("Trophy Catch Plumber"), American Plumbing Heating and Cooling's cartoon owl,
Penguin Air's giant cartoon penguin, Flow Pro Plumbing's cartoon water-drop character paired
with a literal "Spin the Wheel of Real Savings" discount gimmick, and Yell 4 George's illustrated
shouting-man mascot. Explicit coupon-graphic walls were the second most common tell: Absolute
Plumbing (three stacked coupon cards), Top Tier Plumbing & Air and JMAC Plumbing & Air (both
"Current Promotions" dollar-off card rows despite otherwise refined dark/gold design languages),
MNS Plumbing (coupon trio plus broken image placeholders), HIS Plumber and Jim Dandy Sewer &
Plumbing (both had a literal "Coupons" nav item and promo banners on top of dated template
design). The Pink Plumber redirected to a national franchise site (Peachtree Service Experts)
that read as corporate-template rather than hand-built. Black Diamond Plumbing and WaterSmart
Systems leaned on stock lifestyle/product photography rather than real business identity.
Impetus Plumbing & Heating, Cherry Blossom Plumbing, and Red Lilly Plumbing all had genuinely
nice design elements (real photo grids, a hand-illustrated house cutaway, a striking century-
anniversary hero portrait respectively) but their full-page captures hit extensive broken/blank
lazy-load sections — more empty space than content — and were dropped rather than judged
incomplete. Guest Plumbing & HVAC (Ontario) and Wischmeyer's Plumbing Plus were solid-enough
real small-business sites but read more generic/dated-template than the keepers above. Cabrillo
Plumbing shared a template family with two keepers (O'Leary, Water Heater Company) but was
thinner on real photography and was cut to avoid redundancy. Mylo Plumbing & Heating had almost
no real content — a single amateurish photo and an empty reviews section.

Several candidates were bot-blocked (Cloudflare/JS challenge or 403) and not retried since the
trade already had a healthy surplus: Edwards Plumbing. Roscoe Brown (Murfreesboro) failed on a
certificate error. Midcity Plumbers was a parked domain. Fix Your Drip and Cooper Jones Plumbing
both failed to load in the capture pass and were not retried.
