# homebuilders — design reference notes

20 kept out of 23 candidates captured (3 pre-existing: castle-homes, domus-custom-builders,
scott-simpson-design-build; 20 new candidates sourced via a curated agency-portfolio roundup
of real client sites, spread across TX, WA, WI, ME, CO, PA, FL, VA, MD, NV, UT, CT, and AB
Canada). Sourcing was open to any real business worldwide with a genuine, premium, hand-built
site — quality and design discipline were the bar, not geography — so the one Canadian site in
the candidate pool was kept rather than screened out.

- **vitruvius-built-full.png** — Park City, UT. THE benchmark of this set. Pure black-and-white
  editorial system, stunning real architectural photography of mountain estates, distinctive
  black full-bleed content blocks ("What We Build" / "Where We Build" / "How We Work" / "The
  Vitruvius Standard") instead of card grids, real press logos (Luxe, WHJ, Mountain Living, AD
  PRO Directory). Reads like a magazine feature, not a lead-gen site — the top of the tier.
- **altmann-builders-full.png** — Reno, NV. Pure portfolio-grid layout, 14 named real projects
  (Diamond J Upper, Flowering Sage, Saddlebow, Taos, etc.) shot with the same premium
  architectural photography discipline as Vitruvius. Real license number and address in the
  footer. Minimalist, gallery-first, no filler copy.
- **barrett-made-full.png** — Portland, ME. Similar gallery-first format — 40+ real project
  photos (houses, a restaurant build-out, an office, a gym) with almost no marketing copy.
  Distinctive architecture-firm identity rather than a typical contractor template.
- **horizon-building-full.png** — Northern Virginia (Fairfax/Loudoun/Arlington/Alexandria).
  Black + orange, real 8-photo project strip, BuildZoom award badges, a real "Turn-Key Luxury
  Homes" listing with specs, a genuine client-story project page, a real 39-review Google
  carousel with named clients and dates, deep FAQ accordion. Most complete/comprehensive site
  in the set.
- **darling-construction-full.png** — Vero Beach / Jupiter Island / Windermere, FL. Teal accent
  on white, real luxury estate and pool photography (including a candid kids-in-the-pool shot,
  not staged), "Core Values" section, real map + contact block. High-end coastal Florida
  builder identity.
- **birchwood-construction-full.png** — Harbor Springs, MI (Northern Michigan). Distinctive
  voice ("We Build Cool Sh!t"), navy + tan, real crew fist-bump photo, real project photos, a
  named homeowner testimonial, and a genuinely personal "Behind the Build" blog (community
  garden, family stories). Personality-driven counter-example to the polished-corporate norm.
- **cottonwood-custom-builders-full.png** — Boulder, CO (with a Steamboat Springs office).
  Forest green + cream, real 5-project photo grid, named client testimonial ("Paula D., Boulder
  CO"), Boulder-area green-building partner logos. Deliberate, quiet, premium.
  eco-conscious branding done with restraint.
- **lbk-design-build-full.png** — Doylestown, PA (Bucks County). Navy + cream, real "Dream It /
  Build It / Love It" three-step section with real hands-on process photos, three named client
  testimonial cards, a real community-events calendar section. Strong, consistent color system
  across a long page.
- **fortin-construction-full.png** — Maine, family-owned since 1990. Navy/tan/copper, real
  founder-team employee spotlight, real interior kitchen photo, hand-drawn-style service icons,
  genuine blog content. A touch more traditional/homey than the top tier but fully real.
- **emmer-development-full.png** — Gainesville, FL area, "65 years of excellence." Navy + gold,
  real community-development photography (Stoneridge community sign, sports courts), 6-photo
  real interior grid. Builder/developer hybrid — reads slightly more corporate than a boutique
  custom builder but genuinely produced.
- **dahl-custom-homes-full.png** — Cedar Rapids / Iowa City / Marion, IA. Clean serif
  typography, real founder-couple photo (Ben & Keri), real project photos, live Instagram feed.
  Minimal, boutique, unhurried pacing.
- **cm-home-builders-full.png** — Eau Claire, WI. Blueprint-and-dream hero imagery, real
  interior project gallery, named client testimonials, a real community give-back section,
  award badges. Busier than the top tier but genuinely real throughout.
- **bh-design-build-full.png** — Berks/Chester County, PA. Teal + cream, real interior photos,
  a named client testimonial (Steve and Julie), a "100+ projects" stat. Shorter page, thinner
  content than the leaders, but clean and real.
- **the-burnsteads-full.png** — Bellevue, WA (Seattle area). Elegant navy serif branding and
  genuinely magazine-quality interior photography, but the page itself is short — essentially a
  landing page for active communities rather than a full service site. Kept for the photography
  quality; noted as thin.
- **paddy-creek-builders-full.png** — Southern Maine coast. Gray/white minimal palette, a real
  3-category grid (Renovation/Restoration/New Construction), a named client testimonial (Dana &
  Andrea Graichen), press logos (Maine Home + Design, Houzz, Cottage Journal), an 8-photo real
  work gallery.
- **enhanced-homes-full.png** — Arlington, VA. Navy + yellow, real available-home listings with
  real addresses on an interactive map, real portfolio photo grid, named client testimonials
  with neighborhoods (Alex M., Drew P., Jordan T.), FAQ. Real-estate-developer/builder hybrid.
- **grandscape-homes-full.png** — Calgary, Alberta, Canada. Clean, deliberate brand system and
  real project photography with a confident, restrained layout. The only non-US site in this
  set; kept purely on design merit since the sourcing bar is quality/authenticity, not
  geography.

Also kept (already present before this pass): **castle-homes-full.png**,
**domus-custom-builders-full.png**, **scott-simpson-design-build-full.png**.

## Rejected but worth knowing why
**ch-newton** and **david-rogers-homes** both had strong premium hero photography but the
full-page capture came back almost entirely blank below the first viewport (a rendering/lazy-
load failure, not a real design flaw) — worth a re-capture with a longer settle time if
revisited. **watermark-design-build** failed to load at all (`ERR_CERT_COMMON_NAME_INVALID` —
an expired/misconfigured TLS cert on their end).

No candidates in this trade were bot-blocked outright — the three losses above (two broken
captures, one dead cert) were the full extent of the drop rate, well within the usual range
for this sourcing method.
