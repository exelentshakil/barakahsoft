# roofers — design reference notes

14 kept out of 28 candidates captured (sourced via roundup articles + agency portfolio
mining, spread across CA, AZ, GA, OH, LA, NV, FL, PA, NJ, TX, IN, and a Midwest multi-state
operator). All confirmed US-based — real US city/state in the footer NAP and US phone format
on every kept site.

- **guardian-roofing-full.png** — THE benchmark (product owner-validated). Navy + gold as the
  only two brand colors, used identically in every section: nav, buttons, badge circles, map
  pins, footer. Real crew photo and a drone shot of an actual roof job (not stock). Six-card
  service grid with one card visually promoted (yellow "Request Your Estimate" tile breaks the
  grid rhythm on purpose). Financing block, testimonial cards, and a real service-area map all
  read as one deliberate system.
- **janney-roofing-full.png** — Florida (Orlando/Tampa/Jacksonville). Black/white/orange with
  real founder headshot + signature block ("Derek Janney, Founder"), a genuinely large real-photo
  grid of completed roofs (filterable by shingle/tile/metal), and an awards-badge row that reads
  earned rather than purchased. Closest in polish to the Guardian benchmark in this set.
- **innovative-roofing-usa-full.png** — Midwest multi-state. Dark-mode sections with a glowing
  radial-gradient "100%" stat ring and a circular core-values diagram — the most visually distinct,
  modern treatment in the set (navy/gold, not the usual red-white template). Real founder photo,
  financing/discount banners kept restrained rather than becoming a coupon wall.
- **vincents-roofing-full.png** — Texas/Louisiana, family-run since 1994. Olive-green + gold,
  genuinely minimal card grid, real aerial commercial-roof photography, and real Yelp-attributed
  testimonials with names. Understated rather than loud — good counter-example to the
  badge-heavy majority.
- **southern-roofing-exteriors-full.png** — Dalton, GA (Northwest Georgia). Orange + navy carried
  through every section, real drone photography of actual client roofs, and a real yard-sign photo
  ("Another Roof Paid for by Insurance") that reads authentic rather than staged. "People Over
  Profits" messaging feels earned, not corporate.
- **garcia-roofing-full.png** — Gulf South (New Orleans/Baton Rouge/Mississippi), 30 years in
  business. Navy + orange, real founder/owner photo, real family lifestyle photo in the hero,
  "roof now pay later" financing block, 700+ five-star reviews section. Consistent color
  discipline throughout a long page.
- **dl-roofing-full.png** — Las Vegas, NV. Green/black/white with a numbered-step layout (01-07)
  instead of card grids — good reference for an alternative section rhythm. Real drone roof
  photography, real crew photo, Expertise.com award badge.
- **philadelphia-quality-roofing-full.png** — Philadelphia, PA. Red/black/white, real drone
  rooftop-deck photography, a 360°-view feature callout, and before/after cards. Video
  testimonial thumbnails instead of plain text quotes.
- **rjw-exteriors-full.png** — Lake Hopatcong, NJ (windows/doors/roofing combo). Navy + red, real
  team photo, a genuine before/after door-replacement comparison slider, and a clean
  ratings-badge row (Angi/Google/Facebook) that doesn't tip into badge soup.
- **bq-roofing-full.png** — Cincinnati, OH. Split-diagonal hero photo treatment with the logo
  centered on the seam — a distinctive, hand-built-feeling opener. Blue/orange system, circular
  service icons, real crew action photos.
- **jj-roofing-co-full.png** — Pasadena, CA. Blue/orange, real crew group photo in matching
  branded hoodies, stat blocks (98% would refer, 100% rated professional), real Yelp review
  cards. Solid if slightly more template-adjacent than the top tier.
- **copp-roofing-full.png** — California (statewide) + Phoenix, AZ. Gold/black, real before/after
  photo pairs, award badge cluster, service-area map. A bit more badge-heavy than the top
  examples but the gold discipline and real job photography carry it.
- **able-roof-full.png** — Columbus, OH. Maroon/orange/cream, real founder-team photo, named
  Google review, 400,000+ customers stat. Icon-and-text "why choose us" blocks give it a calmer,
  more editorial rhythm than the usual 3-card layout.
- **stay-dry-roofing-full.png** — Indianapolis, IN. Navy/orange/cream, real founder photo with
  signature, GAF President's Club recognition, "Roofs for Troops" community-give-back photo.
  Real service-area map instead of a generic graphic.

## Rejected but worth knowing why
Coupon/badge-wall sites (Phil DiBello Family Roofing, All County Exteriors) were the clearest
"AI slop" tells — stacked $-off coupon cards and manufacturer-badge soup crowding out real
photography, the same pattern flagged in the previous round. Bluebonnet Roofs and Alfrey Roofing
had genuinely real photos but read dated/unrefined (default-template spacing, a random cartoon
mascot image) rather than premium. Scudder Roofing leaned on glossy stock-style renders instead
of real photography. Force Field Roofing, Rogers Roofing, and Lindholm Roofing were otherwise
promising but the full-page capture hit broken/blank lazy-load sections (large solid-color empty
blocks) — worth a re-capture with a longer wait if revisited, since Rogers and Lindholm in
particular had genuine design merit in the sections that did render. Tadlock Roofing's capture
was mostly blank white space after the hero and was dropped rather than judged incomplete.

Five candidates were bot-blocked and not retried since the trade already had a healthy surplus:
Houston Roofing & Construction, Indy Roof Company, Moss Roofing (Sucuri firewall — geo-blocked
non-US traffic, ironic given the US-only mandate), The Roof Joker, and Sutter Roofing.
