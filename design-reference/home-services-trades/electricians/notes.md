## Rejected but worth knowing why
The clearest "AI slop" tells repeated across this trade: literal coupon-graphic walls (My
Electrician FL's "$50 OFF / FREE / $199" trio, Absolute Plumbing-style stacked discount cards on
Any Hour Services, Trutec Electric's "$50 OFF" block paired with illustrated/vector van graphics
instead of real photography); cartoon mascots as primary branding (Dutton Plumbing/"Express
Electrical Services" redirect); and manufacturer/award badge walls crowding out real work
(Brandon Electric's 11+ stacked award badges). Atkins Electric and Branover Contractors both read
as cheap directory-template builds (generic stock worker photography, cartoon avatar
"testimonials," LinkNow/coupon-link footers). Gator Electric had genuinely real jobsite photos but
the rest of the page was an overly formulaic SEO lead-gen template (repeated red CTAs, FAQ walls)
that didn't read as hand-built. New Electric (Oakville, ON) was a well-designed industrial
contractor site but was dropped for being thinner on real photography relative to the keepers
above once the field was already full. Davis Electric (Austin/San Antonio) and JTM Electrical
(Chicago) were both solid real-photo sites but each had one broken/unrendered element (an empty
image box, a lazy-load text bug) and lost out to cleaner captures once the pool exceeded target.
4R's Electric (Philadelphia) and Kollmann Electric (Fond du Lac, WI) were genuine, real small-
business sites but read more dated/template-adjacent than the top tier and were cut once the
keep list ran past 20.

Several candidates were bot-blocked (403/Access Denied) and not retried since the trade already
had a healthy surplus: Deltron Electric FL, Electrician Techs, James Stone Electric, Salt Service
Co, and Louis Shiffman Electric. Residential Electric (MI) and Puckett Electric returned
blank/404 captures and were dropped rather than judged incomplete.
