# karting-recreation — design reference notes

3 kept out of 5 candidates. This category was thin from the start (only 2 seed URLs in the
approved plan) and stayed thin after real sourcing effort — indoor karting/entertainment-venue
websites skew heavily corporate-franchise, and the one B2B track-builder site found
(360 Karting) turned out to be a sales site for venue operators, not a consumer-facing design
reference. Worth a dedicated Google-Maps-by-city pass later ("best go kart racing near
[city]") rather than more roundup-article research, since national chains dominate the
generic-search results.

- **k1-speed-full.png** — Best of the three. Genuine first-person POV action photography in the
  hero (a real kart racer's view of the track, not a stock render), bold red/black/white system
  carried through news tiles and footer, playful real news-tile graphics ("70 Years of Go-Kart
  Racing," "Back to School," "Freedom 250") that read as an in-house design team's work, not a
  template. Good reference for "real action photography + confident brand color" in the
  recreation/entertainment space.
- **rpm-raceway-full.png** — Bold red/black, real event and party photography throughout
  (birthdays, corporate groups, food), a real customer Instagram-style photo grid near the
  footer, consistent card treatment for the 4-step "How it Works" section. The "Customer
  Reviews" section rendered empty on capture — worth a recapture if this one gets used as a
  primary reference.
- **andretti-karting-full.png** — Kept despite a broken/unloaded hero (gray placeholder where a
  video should be). The visible body of the page is genuinely premium: neon-lit real action
  photography for karting/arcade/food in a 3-card layout, diagonal lime-green accent stripes cut
  across navy backgrounds (a distinctive shared motif, not a generic card grid), real candid
  photography of guests in the "More Than a Venue" strip instead of stock people.

## Rejected
360 Karting (360karting.com) is a B2B site selling track-building services to venue operators,
not a consumer-facing karting business — mostly walls of text with large empty gaps between
sparse sections, not useful as a "genuinely premium local business" reference despite being a
real, legitimate company. Pole Position Raceway's domain (polepositionraceway.com) no longer
resolves (DNS failure) — likely defunct or rebranded; worth a fresh search if this category needs
more candidates later.
