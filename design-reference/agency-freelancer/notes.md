# agency-freelancer — design reference notes

13 kept out of 20 candidates captured. Highest baseline-quality category so far — these are
working design/branding studios, so a bad design would actively hurt their own pitch. Best source
for confident typography, restraint, and dense-but-organized portfolio grids.

- **pentagram-full.png** — The reference for "let the work be the entire design system."
  Dense, colorful, varied project grid with almost no chrome around it — logotype, case study
  title, thumbnail, repeat. Proves a portfolio page doesn't need elaborate layout tricks if the
  work itself is strong and consistently presented.
- **alright-studio-full.png** — Extremely varied real campaign photography (streetwear billboard,
  wine toast, formalwear editorial, desert billboard, macro rooster shot) held together purely by
  consistent image framing/spacing — a good reference for how much visual variety a single grid
  rhythm can absorb without feeling chaotic.
- **collins-full.png** — Dark maroon/near-black background, elegant serif headline ("Rewrite your
  worth"), vivid illustrated case-study tiles (Bose, Arcane). Strong example of a "editorial,
  confident, sparse" agency register as opposed to a dense grid.
- **and-walsh-full.png** — Vertical scroll of real, vibrant client work (Plenty produce packaging,
  a painted building mural, Bombas street ad) each labelled with a small caption instead of a
  heavy case-study card — minimal chrome, maximum photography.
- **porto-rocha-full.png** — Very large, dense masonry portfolio grid from one of the most
  awarded branding studios working today; useful for how a long single-page scroll of thumbnails
  can still feel curated via consistent sizing/spacing rules.
- **dixonbaxi-full.png** — Bold black wordmark + "Ask us anything" conversational headline,
  horizontally-scrolling "NEW WORK" tray of real production photography (billboard, TikTok promo,
  film still) each tagged with a pill label — a nice pattern for a horizontal-scroll work strip.
- **clay-full.png** — Real, recognizable client logo row (Slack, Snapchat, Yahoo Games) plus a
  genuinely diverse case-study grid (giraffes for a wildlife-park rebrand, a coffee-shop lifestyle
  shot, crypto/finance app UI) — good reference for mixing photography and UI screenshots in one
  consistent grid.
- **humaan-full.png** — Confident stat callouts (100% in-house, 15 years, 80+ awards) paired with
  a browser-frame showcase of a real shipped site, plus a genuine team photo instead of stock
  people. Good "results + humans" balance for an agency about page.
- **vmv-studio-full.png** — Dense two-column portfolio grid mixing product photography, motion
  stills, and brand identity work; dark sophisticated palette; a real founder portrait breaks up
  the grid rhythm partway down instead of only appearing on an About page.
- **ubiquitous-full.png** — Dark navy/purple gradient dashboard-style hero (data visualization,
  not a photo), real recognizable brand-logo row (Netflix, Amazon, Disney), colorful gradient
  case-study cards (Lyft, BlueChew, Jinx). Good reference for a data/SaaS-adjacent agency register.
- **rno1-full.png** — Bold oversized wordmark distortion effect in the hero, dark background,
  real project screenshots (signage, laptop mockups) in a simple 2-col grid, confident partner-logo
  wall (dentsu, Interos).
- **mixd-full.png** — Public-sector-focused digital agency. Bold yellow/black palette (unusual
  for this space, memorable), real photography of actual client work (kids on a playground,
  healthcare workers), NHS logo as a trust signal, numbered 3-step process cards. Good example of
  a values-forward, non-generic agency page for a less glamorous niche (government/NHS clients).
- **conspire-agency-full.png** — Shopify-focused agency. Real, labelled case-study screenshot
  grid (Zia Tile, Cousins Maine Lobster, Kinto, Portola Paints) is the standout pattern here — a
  clean, scannable "our case studies" tile grid worth reusing even though the rest of the page
  (FAQ accordion, generic orange CTAs) is more template-standard.

## Rejected
That Lot (SNI/TLS error, dead capture), Mr. President (broken lazy-load, mostly empty yellow),
Work & Co (an un-dismissable Accenture cookie-consent modal obscured the page), Beetroot (mostly
empty black sections, too text-only to be a useful visual reference), Pocketknife (large broken/
empty white blocks lower on the page), The Boathouse Agency (mostly empty white gaps between
sparse sections). Active Theory failed entirely — its WebGL intro renders a "Your browser is not
supported" screen under headless Chromium; worth a manual real-browser recapture later since the
studio is genuinely known for premium motion work.
